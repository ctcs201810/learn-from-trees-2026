關於這個活動What is Learn from Trees

「以樹為師」是由攀樹教練與各地樹木工作者，自 2023 年起自主串連的交流活動。從最初的教練交流，逐漸發展成各縣市在學校、社區與不同場域，於同一天各自舉辦的樹木活動——沒有中央主辦單位，只有共同的一天與共同的信念。

Learn from Trees began in 2023 as an informal, grassroots gathering among tree-climbing instructors and local tree workers. It has grown into a decentralized nationwide event: each county hosts its own independent session — in schools, communities, and public spaces — all on the same day.

今年的共同子題是「照顧好現有的樹木，和種下新的樹一樣重要」。我們想把一個容易被忽略的問題帶進公共討論：當社會持續投入資源種下新樹的同時，都市與道路裡已經存在的樹木，是否也被好好保留、照護與管理？

This year's shared theme: caring for the trees we already have is as important as planting new ones. Alongside Taiwan's push to plant and green, we want to bring public attention back to the trees already standing among us.

各地的樣子One theme, many places — 2023–2025

從都市校園到山間村落、水岸咖啡園、大學草地——每年同一天，各地的夥伴用同一面帆布、同一件紫衣、同一塊手拿牌，爬上屬於自己那棵樹。
From schoolyards to mountain villages, riverside coffee groves and university lawns — on the same day, climbers everywhere use the same flag, the same purple shirt and the same hand-held sign, each climbing their own tree.

邀請國際夥伴An invitation to friends overseas

「以樹為師」歡迎世界各地的攀樹與樹藝夥伴，以任何形式一起參與 9 月 26 日這一天。形式完全自由：
Learn from Trees welcomes tree climbers and arborists everywhere to take part on September 26, in any form you like:

  • 同一天或同一週，爬一棵近旁的樹，拍一段給台灣的短短問候
    Climb a nearby tree the same day or week, and film a short greeting to us in Taiwan from up in the canopy.
  • 用共同的社群 hashtag，在自己的地方響應
    Take part where you are, using our shared social-media hashtag.
  • 交換一份小小的紀念品，作為連結的信物
    Exchange a small keepsake as a token of connection.
  • 把你的攀樹地點，加進「以樹為師」的全國地圖
    Add your climbing spot to the Learn from Trees map.

Wherever you climb, we'd love to connect — up in the trees — this September. Reach out any time at ctcs201810@gmail.com, or follow along on Facebook.
